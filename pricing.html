What "unlimited" covers

The daily quota

Unlimited comes with a generous daily quota that most creators never come close to. If you reach it, take a break and come back. It keeps things fast for everyone and favours quality over spam.

Before you subscribe.

Quick answers about plans, billing and cancelling.

All questions
Is there a free trial?

There is no trial period for paid plans. The Free plan gives you access to all tools with pay-as-you-go credits, so you can try the studio before you subscribe.

Is unlimited really unlimited?

Pro and Max include unlimited Apex images and Apex Video Motion. Other video from partner models uses credits on every plan. A generous daily quota applies, set so most creators never come close to it, and speed may adjust during peak demand. Fair-use policies apply to maintain quality service for all users.

How does pay-as-you-go work on the Free plan?

Free gives you full access to all tools and features. Generations are pay-as-you-go with standard queue priority, and credit costs vary by generation type and quality settings. Upgrade for unlimited Apex images and faster processing.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept debit and credit cards for all subscriptions. USDC (crypto) is also available for credit purchases.

How do I cancel, and can I get a refund?

Go to your plan settings and select the Free plan. Your subscription is canceled and you switch to Free at the end of your current billing period, so access continues until then. No refunds are issued for subscriptions or credit purchases, but credits are refunded automatically for failed generations caused by platform errors. Plans auto-renew unless canceled, so cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid being charged. See section 4 of the Terms of Service.

My payment failed. What can I do?

Clear your browser cookies and try a different card. If it still fails, ask your bank to authorize recurring international charges from "MEGABERRY" / "Authorize.net", raise your international transaction limit and enable international online purchases. If a payment is declined repeatedly, wait at least an hour before trying again, then contact support.
