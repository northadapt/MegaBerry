FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about avatar-first creation in Studio.

All questions
What makes MegaBerry different from other AI generators?

MegaBerry locks identity first. Facial and body likeness stay fixed while you control hair, eyes, expression, outfit, pose, props and style, so your character does not mutate from shot to shot.

Can I create with a real person or celebrity likeness?

No. Creating content using another person's likeness without their consent is prohibited under MegaBerry's Acceptable Use Policy. Only verified avatar owners can generate content with their trained identity.

Can I use generated content commercially?

Yes. You own the content you generate with your avatar and may use it commercially, subject to the Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy.

Does MegaBerry support bulk creation?

Yes. Bulk actions let you run many generations at once, and batch size grows with your plan: standard on Free, 10x larger on Pro and 50x larger on Max.

What's the difference between Pro and Max?

Max adds the fastest generation priority, the largest bulk batches, advanced avatar and video features, and the ability to train your own avatar.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period and you will not be charged again.
