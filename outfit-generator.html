Tips & Help

  • Use Random for any field to discover new styles and combinations you might not have considered.
  • Select a Category first. Subcategory and Article options will update based on your choice.
  • Combine Material + Pattern for more realistic textures (e.g., Silk + Floral, Denim + Solid).
  • Secondary colors work best for accents, trims, or two-tone designs.
  • Use Embellishments sparingly. They add detail but can overwhelm simple garments.

Tips & Help

  • Be specific about garment type: "red silk midi dress" works better than just "dress".
  • Include material and texture details (silk, leather, knit, velvet) for realistic results.
  • Specify the shot type (flatlay, hanging, folded, or on-model) for consistent framing.
  • Add lighting and background cues (studio lighting, white marble) for professional results.
  • Keep prompts concise and focused. Shorter, well-crafted prompts often produce better quality.
  • Template mode preserves studio styling. Use Custom mode for complete control.
