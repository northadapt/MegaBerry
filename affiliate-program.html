How This Works

Earn commission on every payment from the people you refer — subscriptions and credit packs.

  1. Share your referral linkPromote your unique URL on social media, your website, newsletters, or anywhere your audience is
  2. They sign up and payWhen someone signs up through your link and makes their first payment (a subscription or a credit pack), it counts as a conversion
  3. You earn commissionYou earn a percentage of every payment they make: subscription payments and renewals, and credit pack purchases. Higher tiers unlock higher rates for future payments

Card payments are held for 30 days to cover refunds and chargebacks before the commission becomes available; a refunded or charged-back payment reverses its commission.

COMMISSION TIERS

Your tier is set by your number of paying referrals. Each payment earns the rate of your tier at the time of that payment.
